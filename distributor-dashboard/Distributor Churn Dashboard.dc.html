How to read this dashboard
1Start with the Risk queue. It lists the operators that need a review, biggest revenue at risk first.
2Open an operator. The page reads top to bottom: what changed, where, why, and the facts.
3Use the review brief before you talk to them. It lists facts and questions — never a price.
Words used on these screens
Risk scoreThe model's estimate (0–100%) that this operator cuts spending with us sharply in the next 90 days. High / Medium / Low riskScore bands. High = likely to cut spending sharply. Medium = early warning signs. Low = buying normally. Revenue at riskWhat the operator usually spends with us in a quarter (Oct–Mar), multiplied by their risk score. Used to decide who to look at first. {{ g.label }}{{ g.hint }} Who should reviewThe team best placed to act, chosen from what changed: Sales, Category team, Pricing or Customer service. Last quarterJul–Sep 2026 compared with Apr–Jun 2026. "Before" means Oct 2025 – Mar 2026.
